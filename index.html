Drain Cleaning in Thornton: A Homeowner's Checklist

Most slow drains give warning before they block. Work through this list to tell a single clog from a main line problem.

Only one drain is slow

A single slow sink, tub or shower is usually a local clog: hair and soap in bathrooms, grease and food in the kitchen. Clear the stopper, use a plunger with the overflow covered, or a hand snake for bathroom drains.

Skip the chemical cleaners

Caustic drain cleaners rarely reach a clog further down the line, and they can damage older pipe and leave standing chemicals for whoever opens the drain next.

Several drains slow at once

If more than one fixture drains slowly, a toilet gurgles when the washer empties, or water comes up the basement floor drain, the blockage is in the main line, not a fixture. That needs a cable machine or a camera, not a plunger.

Older lines in South Thornton

Homes in the 80229 area often still have older sewer laterals where roots get in at a clay joint. Repeated main line clogs in those homes are worth a camera check before anything else.

Heavy buildup

Grease and scale that keep coming back may need hydro jetting rather than another snake run.
